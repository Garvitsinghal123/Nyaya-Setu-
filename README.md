Citizen Shield — Nyaya Setu
Citizen Shield (Nyaya Setu) is a simple, citizen-friendly web application designed to help people understand their basic rights.
The goal is to make important legal and procedural information easier to understand.

Problem statement-Many people are unfamiliar with their basic rights when interacting with the police.
What their basic rights are
What the police can legally do
What they can ask for
What steps they can take
Which official helpline they should contact.

Our Approach
Instead of using lengthy legal documents, Citizen Shield follows a simple flow:
Choose your situation → Understand your rights → Know what to do → Find help.

Features added
Users can select common police-related situations, such as:
1.Stopped or Question by police.
2.Police Refuse to register an FIR.
3.Police threatened, abused, or assaulted
4.Cyber Crime or online Fraud
5.Domestic Violence
and many more.

To tackle different situations, Citizen Shield provide simple yet effective legal guidance to help you understand your rights and know what to do next.
-> Relevant legal information is presented in simple language instead of requiring users to understand complex legal terminology.
-> The project references the Bhartiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023, India's current criminal-procedure law, which came into force on 1 July 2024. 

 Emergency & Support Helplines:
 The application provides quick access to important support and emergency numbers, including relevant national helplines.
->112 — Emergency Response Support
->100 — Police
->1930 — Cyber Crime Helpline
->15100 — NALSA Legal Aid
->181— Women Helpline
->1098 — Child Helpline

The application have  Responsive Design and works on different devices.
